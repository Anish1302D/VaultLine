# VaultLine: Guardrail-First Autonomous Seller Agent for Machine Commerce
**Techfest 2026-27 (IIT Bombay) · AgentX Challenge 2 – Selling to Machines**

### Abstract

**Problem & Business Context:** As autonomous agents increasingly transact in machine-to-machine commerce, unstructured large language model (LLM) sellers suffer catastrophic vulnerabilities: susceptibility to prompt injections, authority spoofing, ungrounded arithmetic hallucinations, and severe margin bleed. In baseline benchmark evaluations on official challenge starter bots, naive LLM sellers exhibited an unacceptable 60% policy violation rate, frequently selling below acquisition cost or exceeding discount ceilings.

**Guardrail-First Innovation:** VaultLine eliminates these commercial vulnerabilities through a 5-layer architecture governed by the foundational axiom: *"Code decides, the LLM only talks."* Pricing authority is strictly removed from generative models and transferred to deterministic mathematical engines bounded by two zero-trust perimeters.

**Architecture & Trust Boundaries:** At Trust Boundary 1, the Layer 1 Input Guard sanitizes buyer messages, clamps negative quantities, and scans for seven adversarial vectors (prompt injections, authority claims, fake competitor quotes, synthetic budget anchors, deadlines, cost probes, and stalls). It emits a prose-free `BuyerIntent` to Layer 2 (StoreFacts), which computes dual-constraint floor prices with upward ceiling rounding and an epsilon buffer. Layer 3 (Negotiation Engine) computes concession paths via a grid-tuned Boulware curve ($\beta = 1.2$, $\gamma = 0.15$), enforcing reciprocity bonuses, stall freezes, volume tiers ($\ge 3, 5, 10$ units), and bundle pricing with independent line-item floor clamping to eliminate cross-subsidies. Layer 4 (Composer) formats approved decisions into wire JSON. At Trust Boundary 2, Layer 5 (Output Validator) independently audits outgoing payloads against seven mandatory gates: floor clamp, discount ceiling, stock availability, numerical fact grounding, banned phrase filtering, exact arithmetic invariants ($\text{total} = \text{price} \times \text{qty}$), and confidentiality protection. Any violation triggers deterministic safe fallback.

**Empirical Results:** Evaluated across 2,100 negotiations ($\ge 300$ runs per persona) across three starter bots and four adversarial LLM personas (Cooperative, Hard Bargainer, Manipulator, Injector), VaultLine achieved **zero policy violations** (100% compliance with HC-1..HC-8), a 72.8% deal closure rate (1,528 deals), a 32.87% preserved profit margin, and $256,221.27 in cumulative net profit. Metamorphic fairness tests (ST-1) proved exact cent-for-cent matching quotes across contrasting buyer tones and claims.

**Cost, Feasibility & Impact:** Deployable as an offline Docker container (`VAULTLINE_NO_LLM=1`) with sub-10ms turn latency, VaultLine eliminates cloud inference costs entirely (\$0.00/turn), scaling to thousands of concurrent negotiations on a single CPU core. Enterprise sellers prevent catastrophic inventory underpricing and revenue loss while automating commercial B2B/B2C transactions with absolute mathematical certainty. Future work includes multi-currency clearing and dynamic market indices.
